Troubleshooting

Common hydraulic symptoms and initial checks.

Low Flow

Check pump speed, suction restriction, oil level, inlet air leaks, internal wear, and relief valve bypass.

Low Pressure

Check relief setting, load, leaks, valve position, pump condition, and gauge accuracy.

Overheating

Check continuous relief flow, cooler performance, viscosity, restrictions, and excessive internal leakage.

Noise / Cavitation

Check inlet vacuum, suction plumbing, aeration, oil temperature, and filtration.

Always depressurize and follow equipment-specific safety procedures before service.

Reference Center

Quick Formulas

Force (lbf) = PSI × area (in²)

Area (in²) = π × diameter² / 4

Flow (GPM) = in³/rev × RPM / 231

Hydraulic HP = PSI × GPM / 1714

Input HP ≈ hydraulic HP / overall efficiency
